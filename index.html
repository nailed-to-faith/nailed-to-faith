Nailed To Faith

Proposal for the Nailed To Faith Platform

nailedtofaith.com

Contact: admin@nailedtofaith.com

A community‑built Christian space for music, testimony, teaching, and decentralised fellowship.

1) Introduction — Why This Platform Exists

The modern internet is loud, crowded, and controlled by corporations. Christian voices often feel drowned out, restricted, or forced to fit into systems that don’t reflect our values. Many believers self‑censor online because they fear judgement, algorithms, persecution, or being misunderstood.

I believe we can build something different.

Nailed To Faith is a simple, independent Christian platform created to share the gospel through music, testimony, teaching, and community. It begins as a small website, but the long‑term vision is a decentralised Christian network — one where the community writes the guidelines, hosts mirrors of this website and shapes the future.

♦ This is not a corporation.
♦ This is not a business.
♦ This is a ministry built by ordinary believers.

2) Who This Platform Is For:

A) Open to All Christian Denominations

NailedToFaith welcomes:

♦ Protestants
♦ Catholics
♦ Orthodox

and all Trinitarian believers

If you believe in the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit as One, you are welcome here.

I grew up in Birmingham England during the 70s and 80s, surrounded by sectarian violence. My husband served in Northern Ireland and still carries the scars. We are never going back to that. Ever!

Inter‑denominational fighting is unchristian and cannot be tolerated. We all sin. Christ teaches us to love the sinner, not the sin. We are not each other’s judge — Christ will resolve our differences when He returns.

B) Open to Everyone — No Login, No Tracking, No Data Collection

The Church is one of the last places on earth where you can walk in without showing ID, giving your address, or even using your real name. Anyone can join a Sunday service. The doors are open.

This platform reflects that same openness.

♦ No email required
♦ No login
♦ No tracking
♦ No analytics
♦ No data collection
♦ No algorithms

Faith should be accessible to all — including those who must remain anonymous for safety. Nailed To Faith supports the persecuted church around the globe. So your security is our priority.

I am producing original Christian music — beginning with a 3‑track EP — soon to be released for free through:

The platform will invite:

♦ musicians

♦ home churches

C) Community‑Written Guidelines Based on Christ’s Teaching

Our template for community guidelines is simple: the 2000 year old teachings of Christ!

Many people misunderstand Christian faith.

♦ We are not here to hate anyone.
♦ We are commanded to love all people.
♦ We hate sin — but we do not hate the sinner.

The community will help refine guidelines that reflect Christ’s compassion, truth, and grace. Community guidlines built by the community, not corporations or algorithms.

D) A Path Toward Decentralisation

Not everyone understands decentralisation, so here is the simple version:

♦ Censorship resistance: No single company or government can silence this platform.
♦ Immutability: Content can be mirrored/hosted and preserved, even if a server disappears.
♦ No overlords: Only God and Christ's inspired followers control this platform’s future, not corporations, not shareholders and not algorithms.
♦ Underground support: Simple HTML pages (like the one you are viewing now) can be copied to USB sticks and shared offline, reaching persecuted Christians where the internet cannot. Keeping things simple and basic is a feature, not a bug.
♦ Resilience: Like the Pirate Bay, which reappeared within days after being taken down, decentralised Christian content can and will survive attempts to suppress it.

This is a slow, organic process — but it begins now.

4) Why I’m Building This

I am 60 years old. I have spent 36 years working in electronics, aviation, manufacturing, and healthcare. I have lived through burnout, hardship, and financial struggle. I now live in rural Bulgaria on €300 per month.

I cannot do physical work anymore. But I can make music. I can write. I can build simple websites. I can create community. I can share the gospel.

This platform is my ministry — the work I want to devote the rest of my life to.
